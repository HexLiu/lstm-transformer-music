# Sources

- Course exercise: [MIT 6.S191 Lab 1, Part 2](https://github.com/MITDeepLearning/introtodeeplearning/blob/master/lab1/PT_Part2_Music_Generation.ipynb).
- Dataset: `mitdeeplearning/data/irish.abc` from the MIT course repository. The exact revision, URL and SHA-256 hash are in `data_source.json`.
- Framework: [PyTorch](https://pytorch.org/).
- ABC parsing and MIDI writing: [music21](https://www.music21.org/).

The dataset is fetched from its original source rather than redistributed in this repository. Generated samples are model outputs and have not been checked for memorized training passages. Refer to the upstream projects for their terms.

This version was reconstructed and rerun on October 19, 2026. The recorded numbers describe this version; they do not recover measurements from an earlier notebook.
